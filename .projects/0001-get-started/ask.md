# 0001 Get Started - The Ask

> Write this BEFORE you open the bot. In your own words. Do not paste Ryan's brief in here.
>
> Restating the ask yourself is the first real test: if you cannot say what you are building
> without looking at his email, you do not understand it yet. Delete this block when you write.

## What I am building

_One paragraph. What is this thing and who is it for?_

## Who uses it

_The three people. For each: why they open it, what they see, and what they must never see._

## What has to work for this box to close

_The portal runs on my machine and I can log in as all three people. Add anything else you
are committing to - but be careful. Everything you add here is something you have to finish._

## What I am deliberately NOT building

_This list matters as much as the one above. It is the one Ryan asks about first at demos:
"What did you decide not to build, and why?"_

## Questions I need answered before I start

_Split them. Be honest about which is which._

**Blocking** - I cannot start until someone answers this.

**Assumed** - I am proceeding this way unless someone tells me otherwise.
