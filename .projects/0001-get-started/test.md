> **"How did you verify this?"** — and "I didn't" means it is not done. Delete this block.

## What I did not get to

_Be honest here. Unknown gaps are worse than known ones, and an honest list is evidence of
judgment. A blank section on a two-week build is not believable._

## How someone else could break this

_Try it. Can a contractor reach something that belongs to the client? Can you change a URL and
see someone else's data? What happens if you submit an empty form?_
