# Feedback — on our instructions, not on your work

This file isn't part of your box. Say the blunt thing.

Where something here is confusing, wrong or missing, that's a problem with the instructions, not with you. Write it down while it's still annoying you — a week later you'll have forgotten, or decided it was your fault.

Fill it in as it happens. Your assistant will offer to log things; let it. Push it with everything else.

---

## 1 · What didn't make sense

A row each time something stopped you, confused you, or turned out to be wrong. *"I couldn't tell whether X meant Y"* is a perfect entry.

| Date | Where | What didn't make sense | How I'd fix it |
|---|---|---|---|
| | | | |

**Where** — the file or the moment: `README.md`, `BRIEF.md`, `CLAUDE.md`, `/team-onboarding`, the PRD gate, a template, or "nowhere, I just didn't know what to do next."

**How I'd fix it** is the useful column. A guess is fine.

Worth recording: an instruction you read twice · two documents that disagreed · a word we used as if you already knew it · something you looked for and couldn't find · a step that assumed a tool or account you didn't have · **a moment you nearly gave up, and what you did instead.**

That last one matters most. Write it down even if you pushed through.
